Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Arzish collects, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. We collect only what the service needs, and we do not sell personal data.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and your name if you add it or sign in with Google.
  • Portfolio information you enter: holdings, amounts, prices, goals, scenarios and settings. You choose what to enter; nothing is pulled from your bank or broker.
  • Notifications and preferences: which alerts you want, alert rules, notifications sent to you, and a daily snapshot of each portfolio's total value used for performance charts and drawdown alerts.
  • Usage records: when you were last active, how many AI questions you asked and their cost, emails we sent you and whether they were delivered.
  • Billing records: your plan, subscription periods and payment amounts. Card details are entered on Safepay's checkout and never reach us.
  • Messages: feedback and questions you send us, and our replies.

How we use it

  • To run the service: save your portfolio, value it with market data, run forecasts and send the notifications you asked for.
  • To answer AI questions: the question, recent conversation and the portfolio details the question needs are sent to the AI service to produce an answer.
  • To manage plans and payments, and to prevent abuse.
  • To support you and improve Arzish, using aggregate figures such as how many people use a feature.
  • To email you about your account, alerts you switched on, the weekly digest (which you can turn off), and occasional product news only if you opted in.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions. Data is stored in Supabase's Mumbai (India) region and protected so each account can read only its own records.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Resend: sends emails.
  • Anthropic: provides the Claude AI models behind the AI analyst. Requests are processed to answer your question and are not used to train models.
  • Safepay: processes card payments.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments. Some of them process data outside Pakistan.

In your browser

Arzish stores your sign-in session and a working copy of your portfolio in your browser's local storage so the app opens quickly and keeps working offline for a moment. If you use Arzish without an account, your portfolio is kept only in that browser. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. Notifications are deleted after 180 days, job logs after 90 days and payment event logs after a year. Billing records may be kept longer where the law requires.

Your choices

  • Download all your data from Account, Profile and data, at any time.
  • Delete your account from the same place. This removes your account, portfolios and history and stops any card subscription.
  • Change which notifications you get, or turn off emails, under Account, Notifications, or with the unsubscribe link in any email.

Children

Arzish is not for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this policy when our practices change and tell you in the app or by email if the change is significant.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: use Help in the app, or the contact address on the home page.
