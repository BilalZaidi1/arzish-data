Plan with the range, not one hopeful number

Pakistan has had rupee crises, 22% policy rates and 30% market falls. Arzish builds them into every forecast instead of assuming a smooth 15% a year.

  • Forecasts in rupees, in dollars or in today’s money after inflation.
  • Goals for school fees, Hajj, a home or retirement, each with its odds and the monthly amount that would secure it.
  • Stress tests that replay a crash or a devaluation on today’s holdings.
  • An optimizer that suggests a mix for your risk level, with Shariah and liquidity limits.
  • Tax and zakat worked out on your holdings, for filers and non-filers.

Questions

Is this investment advice?

No. Arzish shows estimates for education and planning. Forecasts are ranges built from assumptions you can see and change, not promises. For decisions, speak to a licensed adviser.

Do I have to connect my broker or bank?

No. You add holdings yourself, paste a statement, or let the AI read a screenshot; nothing is ever connected to your accounts and Arzish cannot move money.

Where are prices from?

PSX closing prices and indices, daily NAVs published by fund managers through MUFAP, CDNS rates for National Savings, State Bank figures for rates and the rupee, and the Sarafa association for gold. Each figure shows its date and source in the app.

Who can see my portfolio?

Only you. Your data is stored in a private database protected so that each account can read only its own records. We do not sell data. The AI analyst sends the portfolio details a question needs to the AI service to answer it, and nothing is used to train models.

Does it handle Shariah-compliant investing?

Yes. Holdings carry a Shariah status, you can filter to Shariah-compliant assets, and the optimizer can keep a mix fully compliant.

Can I use it from abroad?

Yes. Track Roshan Digital accounts, Naya Pakistan Certificates and property at home alongside investments abroad, and see everything in rupees or dollars.
